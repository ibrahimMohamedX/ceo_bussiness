# PROJEX Brand Voice & Copywriting
Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines how PROJEX communicates.

Every headline, paragraph, button, service description and call-to-action must follow this document.

The goal is to create one recognizable voice across the entire brand.

---

# Brand Position

PROJEX is not a software agency.

PROJEX is not a freelancer team.

PROJEX is an engineering company that designs intelligent digital products.

We combine:

Software Engineering

Embedded Systems

Artificial Intelligence

to solve complex engineering problems.

---

# Brand Personality

PROJEX should sound:

Professional

Confident

Technical

Clear

Modern

Calm

Trustworthy

Innovative

Intelligent

Never sound arrogant.

Never sound childish.

Never sound overly corporate.

---

# Tone of Voice

Our communication should feel like:

An experienced engineering partner.

Not a salesperson.

Not a marketing agency.

We educate.

We simplify.

We inspire confidence.

---

# Writing Principles

Write clearly.

Write directly.

Use short sentences.

Remove unnecessary words.

Avoid buzzwords.

Avoid exaggerated promises.

Explain value.

Not technology for technology's sake.

---

# Brand Promise

We engineer software, embedded systems and AI solutions that are reliable, scalable and built for real-world impact.

---

# Tagline Principles

Taglines should be:

Short.

Technical.

Memorable.

Examples:

Engineering Intelligence.

Building Intelligent Systems.

Engineering the Future.

Where Software Meets Hardware.

Beyond Software.

Intelligence Engineered.

From Silicon to Software.

Embedded. Software. AI.

One Engineering Team.

Engineering Without Limits.

---

# Headline Rules

Maximum:

12 words.

Prefer:

Verb + Outcome.

Examples

Build Intelligent Systems.

Transform Ideas into Engineering Products.

AI That Solves Real Problems.

Engineering Digital Innovation.

Never:

Welcome to our website.

We are the best.

Leading company.

World-class solutions.

---

# Paragraph Rules

Maximum:

2–3 sentences.

Every paragraph should answer:

Why?

How?

Benefit?

Avoid technical jargon unless necessary.

---

# CTA Rules

Buttons should use action verbs.

Preferred

Start Your Project

Book a Discovery Call

Talk to Our Engineers

Explore Our Work

Let's Build Together

View Case Studies

See Our Services

Never use:

Click Here

Learn More (unless secondary)

Submit

Send

---

# Service Descriptions

Every service description should follow this pattern:

Problem

↓

Solution

↓

Business Value

Never only list technologies.

---

# Hero Copy Structure

Eyebrow

Engineering • Embedded • AI

↓

Headline

One powerful sentence.

↓

Description

Explain the value.

↓

CTA

↓

Trust Indicators

---

# Service Headlines

Use verbs.

Examples

Develop Modern Software

Engineer Smart Devices

Build AI Solutions

Automate Operations

Design Embedded Products

---

# Portfolio Copy

Focus on outcomes.

Not implementation.

Bad

Flutter App

Good

Retail Platform That Reduced Checkout Time by 40%

---

# About Page

Talk about:

Mission

Vision

Engineering Culture

Quality

Innovation

Never write company history first.

---

# Blog Voice

Educational.

Practical.

Engineering-first.

Never clickbait.

---

# Error Messages

Friendly.

Professional.

Helpful.

Example

Something went wrong.

Please try again in a moment.

---

# Empty States

Positive.

Simple.

Example

No projects yet.

We're working on something exciting.

---

# Success Messages

Short.

Professional.

Example

Your request has been received.

Our engineering team will contact you shortly.
